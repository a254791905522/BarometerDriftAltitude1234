A privacy card for the altitude shaft.

BarometerDriftAltitude is a free casual game. A hot air balloon drifts in a vertical shaft, burner charges and ballast bags each give one fixed raise, and a target altitude band moves. This card explains the information practice around that flight. It is written for players and families, and it does not replace advice about the law where you live.

This card

The card applies to the BarometerDriftAltitude app on the App Store and to the two pages published with it: the game introduction and this notice. It describes the game as a product. Questions go to the support options on the store listing.

Your Apple ID, the store itself, Screen Time, and any device backup you turn on are Apple services. Their rules remain Apple’s terms and Apple’s privacy policy.

The store privacy label

The App Store privacy section for BarometerDriftAltitude is labeled “Data Not Collected.” The developer reports that the app does not collect data. This card does not describe a tracking stream, an advertising identifier, or a third-party analytics kit beyond that label.

Apple says privacy practices may vary, for example with the features you use or with your age. If the label on the live product page changes, read that label again. This card will be replaced if the practice written here no longer matches the game.

The public label is the check. This page does not invent a hidden event log behind it.

Altitude state on the device

A flight has to keep a few ordinary facts: where the balloon sits in the shaft, where the target band sits, and how many burner charges and ballast bags are still unused. Those facts belong to the installed game on the iPhone, iPad, or other Apple device you are using.

The store label does not present that state as data the developer collects. Delete BarometerDriftAltitude and the local app copy the system kept for it goes with the deletion. If you enable a backup yourself, such as iCloud Backup, Apple may include app data under the backup settings on your Apple ID. The product listing does not describe a separate cloud login for this game.

Sign-in, contacts, and location

You can open the shaft without creating a profile. Before the first burner charge or ballast bag, the game does not ask for an email address, phone number, real name, contact book, photo library, camera, microphone, or precise location.

The decisions that matter are on the screen: when to lift, and whether the next fixed raise comes from a burner charge or a ballast bag. The listing does not describe those taps as a form posted to a publisher server.

2000FT

Burner charges and ballast bags

Each resource raises the balloon by a fixed amount. Using one permanently consumes that unit. They are the two supplies the shaft gives you for reaching the moving band.

The App Store lists the game as free. This card does not describe a publisher account, a stored payment card, or a balance used to refill charges and bags. If Apple shows its own download or payment prompt on your device, that step is Apple’s transaction.

2400FT

Distribution through Apple

BarometerDriftAltitude is obtained from the App Store. Install, update, and refund requests are handled there. The listing is in English, rated 4+, requires iOS 15 or later, and is designed for iPad. It also names iPhone, iPod touch, Mac, and Apple Vision compatibility lines. The Mac line is marked not verified for macOS.

Information Apple already holds because you use an Apple ID stays under Apple’s policy. Installing this game does not copy that information into a publisher profile.

Families and the 4+ rating

The age rating on the store is 4+. A parent or guardian chooses whether the game is installed. Screen Time, Ask to Buy, and Family Sharing can block or allow that install when those Apple controls are on.

Play does not open with a registration form. If you believe a child sent personal information to the publisher outside ordinary play, use support on the App Store product page and include only what is needed to identify the request.
